🛡️ How your circle stays yours

New here? Create your key file above — it is made right here in this browser with its built-in random generator, then downloads straight to you. This page has no server and no account, so your key is never sent, seen, or stored by anyone — there is nothing on the other end to break into.

Every key starts its own circle. Two people with different keys cannot open each other's codes, cannot pass each other's key check, and cannot touch each other's packages — the math keeps circles apart even though everyone uses the same public page. Your files only ever travel directly between your own devices.

One rule: keep your key file private. Anyone holding it can join your circle — the same way anyone holding a house key can open the door. If it ever leaks, create a fresh one and your old circle stops working.
